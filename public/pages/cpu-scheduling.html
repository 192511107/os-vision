Supported Scheduling Algorithms

Theoretical foundations powering the interactive scheduling simulation engine.

◆ First-Come, First-Served (FCFS)

Non-preemptive dispatch strictly ordered by arrival timestamp. Serves as baseline for latency and demonstrates the convoy effect.

◆ Shortest Job First (SJF)

Non-preemptive dispatch selecting the ready task with shortest burst time. Proven to yield minimal average waiting time.

◆ Shortest Remaining Time First (SRTF)

Preemptive variant of SJF where newly arriving tasks with shorter burst times preempt the currently running process.

◆ Round Robin (RR)

Preemptive circular queue scheduling allocating fixed time quantum slices to guarantee fair interactive responsiveness.

◆ Priority Scheduling

Non-preemptive dispatch prioritized by urgency rank (lower numerical value indicates higher dispatch priority).
